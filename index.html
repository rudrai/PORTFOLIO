R.

I'm Rudra, a final-year Computer Science Engineering student who splits time between the code editor and the whiteboard. My focus sits at the intersection of frontend development and product thinking — I like being the person who can prototype the idea, argue for why it matters, and then actually ship it.

That combination has taken shape through projects like Study Flow, a React and Vite productivity dashboard I designed and deployed end-to-end, and through product-management work like DriveFlow, a CRM case study built around lead management, kanban workflows, and automation design.

Most recently I've been going deep on agentic AI — RAG, tool-use loops, multi-agent systems — with an eye toward where product management, design systems, and AI-native interfaces are heading next.
