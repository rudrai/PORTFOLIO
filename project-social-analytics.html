Social Intern — Data Analysis

Performance analysis of social content during an internship, translating raw engagement data into recommendations that shaped posting strategy.

Type

Completed

Overview

During a Social Intern role, I analyzed how content was actually performing across posts — not just surface-level likes, but the patterns underneath: what formats, timing, and topics consistently drove engagement, and what quietly underperformed.

The problem

The team was publishing consistently but making posting decisions on instinct rather than evidence, with no clear read on which content types were actually earning attention versus just going out on schedule.

The approach

I pulled engagement data across the account's post history and looked for patterns by format, timing, and topic, then translated those patterns into plain recommendations the team could act on immediately rather than a dense analytics report nobody would read.

Key features

  • Engagement analysis across post formats and topics
  • Timing and cadence review to identify high-performing windows
  • Plain-language recommendations translated from raw data

Outcome

The analysis directly informed the team's posting strategy going forward. It's also the project that shaped how I think about product decisions generally — start from the data, but always translate it into something a person can actually act on.
