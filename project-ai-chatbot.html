Overview

An AI-powered chatbot built to answer academic questions for students — the kind of quick, specific queries that usually mean digging through a textbook, a syllabus, or a forgotten lecture slide. It explores retrieval-based response design so answers stay grounded in real course material instead of generic guesses.

The problem

Students often need a fast, specific answer to an academic question and don't have an efficient way to find it outside of manually searching notes or waiting on an instructor's reply.

The approach

I built the assistant around a retrieval-first pattern: rather than relying purely on a model's general knowledge, the system pulls relevant academic content first and grounds its responses in that material. This is also the project that kicked off my deeper interest in agentic AI — retrieval loops, tool use, and how these patterns extend into full agent systems.

Key features

  • Conversational interface for academic Q&A
  • Retrieval-based response design grounded in course material
  • Focused on everyday, practical classroom use cases

Outcome

The chatbot works as a functioning prototype and, more importantly, became my entry point into agentic AI concepts — RAG, tool-use loops, and multi-agent patterns — which I've since been studying more formally alongside my frontend and product work.
